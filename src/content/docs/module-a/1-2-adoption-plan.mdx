  Build the evidence pack around a real project plan. The strongest submission is a customer-approved
  plan that already contains cost, timeline, ownership, and delivery-tracking sections.
</Aside>

## Audit Gate

| Control name | What auditors check | Minimum evidence | Pass threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.1.2 Cloud & AI Adoption Plan | A practical adoption plan that tracks milestones, funding, delivery cadence, and engineering readiness for Azure adoption work. Auditors look for planning artefacts tied to cost data and delivery-maturity evidence. | For each of 2 customers: Cost Management export plus Pricing Calculator estimate, DevOps Capability Assessment report, customer-facing adoption plan, and at least one tracking artefact such as a board, status report, or steering dashboard. | 2 unique customers; evidence created within the last 12 months; a passed Module A result can be reused for 2 years. |

## Audit-ready evidence pack

1. Project plan with phases, milestones, decision gates, and owners.
2. Cost model showing estimated Azure spend and migration assumptions.
3. DevOps Capability Assessment results and planned remediation actions.
4. Delivery-tracking artefact proving the plan was actively managed.

## Common fail conditions

- Pricing Calculator output omitted from the plan pack.
- DevOps Capability Assessment treated as optional.
- Plan shows milestones but no cadence for status tracking or governance.
