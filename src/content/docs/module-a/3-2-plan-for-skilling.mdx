<Aside type="note">
  Keep the skilling plan role-based. Auditors want to see who must learn what, through which learning
  path, and how the team will prove completion.
</Aside>

## Audit Gate

| Control name | What auditors check | Minimum evidence | Pass threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.3.2 Plan for Skilling | A customer-facing skilling plan that maps technical roles to required skills, Microsoft Learn content, certifications, and operational handover resources. | For each of 2 customers: skilling plan, role-to-skills matrix, Microsoft Learn and certification mapping, and evidence of supporting handover material such as recordings, runbooks, or Knowledge Transfer (KT) plans. | 2 unique customers; evidence created within the last 12 months; a passed Module A result can be reused for 2 years. |

## Audit-ready evidence pack

1. Role-based skilling matrix for platform, network, security, and operations roles.
2. Microsoft Learn paths and certification targets.
3. Delivery timeline for learning, labs, and follow-up reviews.
4. Linked handover assets such as recordings, runbooks, and Knowledge Transfer (KT) schedule.

## Common fail conditions

- Training catalogue provided with no role mapping.
- Certifications recommended but no learning path or completion plan.
- No linkage between the skilling plan and the operational handover approach.
