## Audit Gate

| Control name | What auditors check | Minimum evidence | Pass threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| A.1.1 Cloud & AI Adoption Business Strategy | A repeatable strategy motion that turns business drivers into Azure adoption recommendations backed by financial and organisational data. Auditors expect a structured review, customer-specific recommendations, and clear business-value outcomes. | For each of 2 customers: FinOps Review export, Cloud Adoption Strategy Evaluator (CASE) output, strategy readout or workshop summary, and a recommendation pack that links actions to cost, risk, or growth outcomes. | 2 unique customers; evidence created within the last 12 months; a passed Module A result can be reused for 2 years. |

## Audit-ready evidence pack

1. Customer business drivers and forcing function.
2. FinOps Review findings with recommended actions.
3. CASE output showing maturity themes and priorities.
4. Customer-facing summary with business outcomes, owners, and next steps.

## Common fail conditions

- FinOps Review missing or not shared with the customer.
- CASE output exported but not translated into recommendations.
- Generic recommendations with no customer-specific business context.
