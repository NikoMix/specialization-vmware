## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that you operate a **defined, data-driven process** for guiding customer cloud and AI adoption decisions. The process must cover: (1) a structured strategy review of customer business needs, (2) personalised recommendations based on that review, and (3) recommendations specifically for maximising cloud business value (financial and non-financial).

**Typical questions:**
- Show us the business-needs review you ran for two customers — what tools, what cadence, what outputs?
- Where is the FinOps Review evidence — the export, the findings, and the recommendations delivered to the customer?
- Where is the CASE (Cloud Adoption Strategy Evaluator) output that drove the strategy recommendation?
- How did the recommendations link to measurable business outcomes for the customer?

## Evidence Guidance

### FinOps Review (required)

- Run via the [Microsoft FinOps Review](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cost-management-billing/finops/) assessment template
- Export the completed assessment as PDF
- Pair with a 1–2 slide customer-facing summary of findings and next actions

### Cloud Adoption Strategy Evaluator (CASE) (required)

- Conduct CASE with the customer's business and IT stakeholders
- Capture the maturity-by-domain output
- Translate findings into a prioritised recommendation list

### Strategy review process

Demonstrate that the customer engagement followed a documented process:

1. Discovery interviews with business + IT stakeholders
2. Workload portfolio review
3. Financial baseline (current state → target state)
4. Recommendation set (technical + financial + change)
5. Stakeholder readout

### Cloud business-value recommendations

Must address both:
- **Financial outcomes** — TCO, ROI, payback period, ACR run-rate
- **Non-financial outcomes** — speed to market, risk reduction, talent attraction, sustainability

## Common Gaps

- **No FinOps Review on file**: This is a *required* assessment, not optional. Without it, A.1.1 fails. Run it as soon as the engagement begins.
- **CASE output not linked to recommendations**: The auditor wants to see how the CASE findings shaped the strategy — not just the raw output.
- **Only financial outcomes addressed**: Make sure non-financial outcomes (risk, speed, sustainability) are explicit.
- **Recommendations are generic**: "Migrate to Azure" is not personalised. Recommendations must reference the customer's specific workloads, constraints, and business drivers.
