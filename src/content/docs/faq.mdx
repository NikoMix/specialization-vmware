## Pre-Qualification

### Is VCP-DCV still required for the audit?

**No.** The previous Module B Control 1.1 (Third-Party Certifications — VCP-DCV / Master Services Competency for Data Center Virtualization) was **removed in the V1.8 update (October 8, 2025)** and remains absent in V1.9.1. VCP-DCV is **recommended** as a delivery skill (your VMware SME should hold it) but is **not an audit gate**.

### How do I know if we already meet the ACR thresholds?

Check **Partner Center → Insights → Azure Revenue** and filter to the relevant time window. Note that Partner Center data typically lags by **2–4 weeks**, so plan accordingly. Your PDM can provide a more current view on request. AVS node consumption (AV36 / AV36P / AV52 / AV64) is the primary anchor.

### Can we count ACR from multiple subscription types (CSP, DPOR, PAL)?

Yes. All three eligible association types count: **DPOR**, **PAL**, **CSP**. A single customer may have multiple association types but counts as one unique customer.

### Can a single individual's certifications count toward the minimum if they hold multiple?

Yes for the AZ-104 / AZ-305 minimum (each cert needs at least one holder). One person holding both AZ-104 and AZ-305 satisfies both lines.

## Audit Process

### How long does the audit take?

For well-prepared partners, the full process from audit request to badge publication is typically **6–8 weeks**. Unprepared partners typically take **12–16 weeks**.

### How much does the audit cost?

- **Module B only**: $2,400 USD, 4-hour session
- **Module A + Module B combined**: $3,600 USD, 8-hour session

The audit is conducted by **ISSI** (Information Systems &amp; Services Inc.).

### When can I skip Module A?

If your organisation passed **Module A within the last 2 years**, you may request **Module B only** at your next renewal. Confirm the prior pass date with your PDM before booking the audit.

### What format does the auditor want evidence in?

Auditors generally accept **PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint** documents. Screenshots should be exported as PDF where possible. All documents should be clearly named with control references (e.g., `B.1.1_AzureMigrate_Assessment_v2.pdf`).

### What happens if we fail the audit?

You receive a **remediation report** itemising the specific controls and gaps. You have approximately **30 days** to address and resubmit. Failure to remediate may require restarting the audit process.

### Can we anonymise customer names in case studies and references?

Yes for internal documents (e.g., "Customer A"). However, ISSI may verify references against Partner Center records. Work with your PDM on acceptable anonymisation levels.

### Is the audit conducted in English?

Yes, by default. Confirm language options with ISSI at kickoff.

## Evidence – AVS specifics

### Do we have to use Azure Migrate, or can we use a 3rd-party tool?

You may use partner / community tools for discovery and dependency mapping, **but the final recommendation must be consolidated into Azure Migrate**. The auditor expects Azure Migrate output as part of B.1.1 evidence.

### Performance-based vs. As-on-premises Azure Migrate assessment — which?

The checklist advises informing customers of both options and selecting based on context. Performance-based gives more accurate AVS sizing for steady-state workloads. As-on-premises is appropriate when performance metrics are unreliable or unavailable.

### What if a workload is flagged "Ready with conditions" or "Not ready"?

Your assessment evidence must include a **remediation plan** for these VMs (e.g., resize, refactor, exclude from wave 1). The remediation plan itself becomes part of the B.1.1 evidence.

### Is ExpressRoute Global Reach mandatory for the audit?

Required **when available** in the regions involved. The design (B.2.1) must reflect Global Reach for migration scenarios where on-prem ↔ AVS connectivity is needed and Global Reach is regionally available.

### Do we need stretched clusters in our reference design?

Not mandatory. Stretched clusters are one of the six reference architectures and apply when the customer needs near-zero RPO across two metro Availability Zones. Most engagements use single-cluster AVS private clouds.

### What counts for B.3.3 (AVS configuration &amp; Azure integration)?

You must demonstrate evidence in **at least 3** of: vSphere Content Library on Azure Blob; licensed Windows/Linux images in Content Library; AD or federated Entra ID via on-prem AD on Azure VMs as AVS identity source; non-impact host add/remove; AVS app gateway; AVS public internet enablement; AVS node scale-out; quota-increase process; Virtual WAN multi-site.

### Does HCX have to be the migration tool?

No. You may use HCX (Enterprise edition is included with AVS), VMware-native motion mechanisms, or 3rd-party tools (Commvault, RiverMeadow, Rubrik, etc.). Whichever you choose must be demonstrated with plans/scripts/output snapshots in B.3.2.

## Post-Specialization

### How long is the Advanced Specialization valid?

Advanced Specializations are typically valid for **one year** and must be renewed annually. The renewal process includes a re-audit or a lighter evidence update, depending on Microsoft's renewal policy at the time. **Module A specifically is reusable for 2 years**, so renewal cycles may alternate between Module B only and combined audits.

### What about the V2.0 PREVIEW (Jun 1, 2026)?

The V1.9.1 checklist is active **Jan 1 – Jun 30, 2026**. A **V2.0 PREVIEW** for both Module A and Module B becomes effective **June 1, 2026**. Plan to re-baseline your evidence package after the current cycle. The Engagement Agent and this guide will be updated when V2.0 is published.

### Where does the specialization badge appear?

On your **Microsoft AppSource partner profile**, in **Microsoft Partner Finder**, and downloadable from Partner Center for use on your website and marketing materials.

### Are there additional benefits beyond the badge?

Yes:
- **Co-sell priority** for AVS opportunities
- **Marketing through Microsoft** (MtM) eligibility
- **Partner-funded marketing** opportunities
- Access to **Microsoft-led go-to-market programmes**

<Aside>
  Benefits and programmes change periodically. Confirm current benefits with your PDM or on the
  [Microsoft Partner Network benefits page](https://partner.microsoft.com/en-us/partnership/partner-network/benefits).
</Aside>
