## Overview

The Advanced Specialization audit is conducted by **ISSI** (Information Systems &amp; Services Inc.), a Microsoft-approved third-party audit firm. The audit is performed remotely (video call + document review) and typically takes **2–4 weeks** from first contact to result, depending on evidence readiness.

| Item | V1.9.1 detail |
|---|---|
| Checklist version | V1.9.1, active **Jan 1 – Jun 30, 2026** |
| Auditor | ISSI |
| Cost – Module B only | $2,400 USD (4-hour session) |
| Cost – Module A + Module B combined | $3,600 USD (8-hour session) |
| Module A reuse | Pass is valid for **2 years** — may waive at next Module B renewal |
| Successor | V2.0 PREVIEW (Module A &amp; B) effective **Jun 1, 2026** |

<Aside type="tip">
  Partners who prepare their evidence package in advance (using this guide) consistently report faster
  audit cycles and fewer remediation rounds. Aim to have all evidence collected **before** requesting
  the audit.
</Aside>

## Audit Phases

<Steps>

1. ### Pre-Qualification Confirmation

   Before requesting the audit, confirm you meet all four [pre-qualification requirements](../requirements/).

   **Actions:**
   - Run the Quick Readiness Checklist on the Requirements page
   - Confirm ACR figures with your PDM (Insights data can lag by 2–4 weeks)
   - Ensure AZ-104 and AZ-305 certifications are active
   - Confirm Module A pass status — if passed within last 2 years, you may opt for Module B only ($2,400 / 4 h)

2. ### Audit Request via Partner Center

   Navigate to **Partner Center → Benefits → Advanced Specializations** and request the audit for _VMware on Microsoft Azure_.

   **What happens next:**
   - Microsoft routes your request to ISSI
   - You receive an introductory email within 5–10 business days
   - A kickoff call is scheduled with your assigned auditor

3. ### Kickoff Call with Auditor

   The kickoff call is typically 30–60 minutes. Expect the auditor to:

   - Introduce the audit process and timeline
   - Confirm which modules will be assessed (Module B only, or A + B combined)
   - Agree on evidence submission format (SharePoint, email, or their portal)
   - Set a deadline for evidence submission

   **Tip:** Bring the AVS migration lead, network architect, and your Microsoft assessment owner (FinOps Review, CASE, ALZ Review, WAR).

4. ### Evidence Collection &amp; Submission

   Use the [Evidence Tracker](../evidence-tracker/) to manage collection across all controls.

5. ### Auditor Review

   ISSI reviews your submitted evidence against the V1.9.1 checklist and may request clarifications or additional documents. Typical review duration: **5–10 business days**.

   - Respond to queries promptly (target &lt; 2 business days)
   - Assign a single point of contact for auditor communication
   - Keep evidence version-controlled (use dated filenames)

6. ### Audit Interview (Module B technical walk-through)

   ISSI typically requests a 4-hour video session for Module B (or 8 hours combined with Module A). Prepare:

   - AVS migration lead to walk through the assessment, design, and migration evidence for the named customer
   - Network architect to discuss ExpressRoute / Global Reach / NSX-T design
   - Microsoft assessment owner to demonstrate FinOps / CASE / WAR / ALZ Review outputs

7. ### Remediation (if applicable)

   If gaps are identified, ISSI provides a **remediation report** listing specific controls and missing evidence.

   <Aside type="caution">
     You typically have **30 days** to address remediation findings. Treat this as a priority — delays
     can result in needing to restart the audit cycle.
   </Aside>

   - Address each finding systematically using the Module A/B pages in this guide
   - Resubmit only the affected controls unless instructed otherwise

8. ### Audit Pass &amp; Specialization Award

   Once ISSI confirms a pass, Microsoft Partner Center is updated within **10–15 business days**. The specialization badge appears on your profile.

   **Next steps after passing:**
   - Download the specialization badge from Partner Center
   - Update your marketing materials and website
   - Notify your PDM to unlock co-sell and marketing benefits
   - **Module A**: log the pass date — you may reuse it for the next 2 years

</Steps>

## Timeline Summary

| Phase | Typical Duration |
|---|---|
| Pre-qualification confirmation | 1–2 weeks |
| Audit request to ISSI assignment | 5–10 business days |
| Evidence collection (prepared) | 1–2 weeks |
| Evidence collection (unprepared) | 4–8 weeks |
| Auditor review | 5–10 business days |
| Audit interview (Module B 4 h / combined 8 h) | 1 day |
| Remediation (if needed) | Up to 30 days |
| Specialization badge publication | 10–15 business days |
| **Total (well-prepared partner)** | **~6–8 weeks** |
