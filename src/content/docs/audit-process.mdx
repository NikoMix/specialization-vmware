## Overview

The Advanced Specialization audit is conducted by **ISSI** (Information Systems & Services Inc.), a Microsoft-approved third-party audit firm. The audit is performed remotely (video call plus document review) and typically takes **2–4 weeks** from first contact to result, depending on evidence readiness.

| Item | V1.9.1 detail |
|---|---|
| Checklist version | V1.9.1, active **Jan 1 – Jun 30, 2026** |
| Auditor | ISSI |
| Cost – Module B only | $2,400 USD (4-hour session) |
| Cost – Module A + Module B combined | $3,600 USD (8-hour session) |
| Module A reuse | Pass is valid for **2 years** and may waive at the next Module B renewal |
| Successor | V2.0 preview effective **Jun 1, 2026** |

  Use the new [End-to-End Project Plan](../engagement/project-plan/) as the master delivery schedule.

  happening instead of trying to reconstruct it later.
</Aside>

</Aside>

## Audit Phases

### 1. Pre-Qualification Confirmation

   Before requesting the audit, confirm you meet all four [pre-qualification requirements](../requirements/).

   **Actions:**
   - Run the quick readiness checklist on the Requirements page.
   - Confirm Azure Consumed Revenue (ACR) figures with your partner-development manager because Insights data can lag by 2–4 weeks.
   - Ensure AZ-104 and AZ-305 certifications are active.
   - Confirm Module A pass status. If it was passed within the last 2 years, you may opt for the Module B-only audit.

### 2. Audit Request via Partner Center

   Navigate to **Partner Center → Benefits → Advanced Specializations** and request the audit for _VMware on Microsoft Azure_.

   **What happens next:**
   - Microsoft routes your request to ISSI.
   - You receive an introductory email within 5–10 business days.
   - A kickoff call is scheduled with your assigned auditor.

### 3. Kickoff Call with Auditor

   The kickoff call is typically 30–60 minutes. Expect the auditor to:

   - Introduce the audit process and timeline.
   - Confirm which modules will be assessed.
   - Agree on evidence-submission format.
   - Set the deadline for evidence submission.

   **Tip:** Bring the AVS migration lead, network architect, and your Microsoft assessment owner for FinOps Review, CASE, Azure Landing Zone review, and the Azure Architecture Review assessment.

### 5. Auditor Review

   ISSI reviews your submitted evidence against the V1.9.1 checklist and may request clarifications or additional documents. Typical review duration: **5–10 business days**.

   - Respond to queries promptly (target less than 2 business days).
   - Assign a single point of contact for auditor communication.
   - Keep evidence version-controlled and date-stamped.

### 6. Audit Interview (Module B technical walk-through)

   ISSI typically requests a 4-hour video session for Module B, or 8 hours combined with Module A. Prepare:

   - AVS migration lead to walk through assessment, design, and migration evidence.
   - Network architect to discuss ExpressRoute, Global Reach, and NSX-T design.
   - Microsoft assessment owner to demonstrate FinOps, CASE, Azure Architecture Review, and Azure Landing Zone review outputs.

### 7. Remediation (if applicable)

   If gaps are identified, ISSI provides a remediation report listing specific controls and missing evidence.

   <Aside type="caution">
     You typically have **30 days** to address remediation findings. Treat this as a priority—delays can result in needing to restart the audit cycle.
   </Aside>

   - Address each finding systematically using the module pages in this guide.
   - Update the project plan and evidence tracker before resubmitting so the same gap does not recur.

### 8. Audit Pass and Specialization Award

   Once ISSI confirms a pass, Microsoft Partner Center is updated within **10–15 business days**. The specialization badge then appears on your profile.

   **Next steps after passing:**
   - Download the specialization badge from Partner Center.
   - Update marketing materials and website.
   - Notify your partner-development manager to unlock co-sell and marketing benefits.
   - Record the Module A pass date because it can be reused for the next 2 years.

## Timeline Summary

| Phase | Typical duration |
|---|---|
| Pre-qualification confirmation | 1–2 weeks |
| Audit request to ISSI assignment | 5–10 business days |
| Evidence collection (prepared partner) | 1–2 weeks |
| Evidence collection (unprepared partner) | 4–8 weeks |
| Auditor review | 5–10 business days |
| Audit interview | 1 day |
| Remediation (if needed) | Up to 30 days |
| Specialization badge publication | 10–15 business days |
| **Total for a well-prepared partner** | **~6–8 weeks** |
