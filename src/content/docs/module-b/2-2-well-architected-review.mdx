## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies use of the **Azure Well-Architected Review (WAR)** on AVS workloads, evaluating against pillars relevant to the workload. You must demonstrate output for **at least 2 pillars**. The review may be conducted before, during, or after deployment.

**Typical questions:**
- Show the WAR export for an AVS customer.
- Which 2 (or more) pillars did you cover, and why those?
- How did findings translate into action?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Exported WAR results** for at least 1 unique customer (customer named) covering ≥ 2 pillars | PDF | ⬜ |
| 2 | **Pillar selection rationale** | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 3 | **Action log** linking WAR findings to remediation | PDF, Excel | ⬜ |

> **Customers required:** 1 unique • **Window:** Last 12 months
> **Minimum pillar coverage:** 2

<Aside type="tip">
  For AVS workloads, the strongest WAR pillars are typically **Reliability** (stretched-cluster
  decision, host failure, SLA math), **Cost Optimization** (node reservations, ANF tier, ER
  bandwidth), and **Operational Excellence** (Arc, Monitor, syslog, runbooks). Choose 2 of these
  for richest evidence.
</Aside>

## Evidence Guidance

### Running WAR for AVS

- Run via [Microsoft Assessments → Azure Well-Architected Review](https://learn.microsoft.com/assessments/azure-architecture-review/)
- Scope to the AVS workload (private cloud + guest VMs)
- Select 2+ pillars relevant to the engagement
- Customer must be named on the export

### Pillar selection (AVS-specific guidance)

| Pillar | AVS questions to focus on |
|---|---|
| **Reliability** | Stretched clusters? Host failure tolerance? SLA composition (AVS + ANF + ER)? Backup RPO/RTO? |
| **Cost Optimization** | Node reservation vs PAYG? AV36 vs AV52 vs AV64 right-sizing? ANF tier choice? ER bandwidth scaled to actual use? |
| **Operational Excellence** | Azure Arc coverage of guest VMs? Monitor for AVS deployed? Syslog from ESXi / NSX-T? Runbooks for AVS-specific incidents? |
| **Security** | NSX-T DFW baseline? Defender for Servers on guest VMs? Customer-managed keys (CMK) for AVS? |
| **Performance Efficiency** | vSAN ESA capacity headroom? Storage policies per workload tier? |

### Action log

Each WAR finding → owner → target date → status. Show closure or in-flight tracking.

## Common Gaps

- **Only 1 pillar covered**: V1.9.1 requires **2+**. Re-run to add a second pillar if needed.
- **No customer name on the export**: The auditor needs the customer named on the artifact.
- **No action log**: WAR is a process; the findings must lead somewhere.
