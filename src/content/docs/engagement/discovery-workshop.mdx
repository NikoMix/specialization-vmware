## Format

3 days on-site (or remote-equivalent), customer + partner squad together. Deliverable is a workshop output pack that seeds the HLD, the wave plan, and the WAF assessment.

---

## Day 1 — Business + Estate

| Time | Topic | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Welcome + outcomes for the workshop | PM |
| 09:30 | Business drivers, forcing function, success criteria | Sponsor + PM |
| 10:30 | Review of qualification questionnaire | PM + Customer IT lead |
| 11:30 | vSphere estate deep-dive (versions, hosts, vSAN, NSX-T) | Customer VMware lead + Partner VMware SME |
| 13:30 | Top-20 workload walk-through (criticality, dependencies, owners) | Customer app owners |
| 15:00 | Initial AVS sizing discussion (node SKU candidates) | Partner AVS architect |
| 16:00 | Day 1 recap + Day 2 prep | PM |

**Outputs**

- Validated business drivers
- Top-20 workload list with criticality + dependencies
- Initial AVS node SKU candidates

---

## Day 2 — Networking + Identity + Security

| Time | Topic | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Day 1 recap | PM |
| 09:30 | WAN topology + ER + Global Reach feasibility | Partner network engineer + Customer network lead |
| 10:30 | CIDR overlap check (on-prem / AVS mgmt / AVS workload) | Partner network engineer |
| 11:30 | NSX-T design discussion (T0/T1, DFW baseline, segments) | Partner network engineer |
| 13:30 | Identity (AD forest, Entra, AVS SSO source) | Partner identity SME + Customer AD admin |
| 15:00 | Security posture (Defender for Cloud, Sentinel, DFW, IDS/IPS) | Partner SecOps + Customer CISO delegate |
| 16:00 | Day 2 recap | PM |

**Outputs**

- ExpressRoute / Global Reach decision
- CIDR plan with non-overlap confirmed
- NSX-T high-level design
- Identity binding plan for AVS SSO

---

## Day 3 — Migration waves + DR + Landing zone gaps

| Time | Topic | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Day 2 recap | PM |
| 09:30 | Wave plan — workload sequencing, rollback strategy, freeze windows | Partner migration lead + Customer app owners |
| 11:00 | HCX readiness review (bandwidth, port + MTU prereqs) | Partner VMware SME |
| 13:00 | DR strategy (in-AVS DR, AVS-as-DR, SRM vs HCX DR) | Partner AVS architect + Customer DR lead |
| 14:30 | Landing zone gap analysis (vs CAF ALZ + AVS LZ Accelerator) | Partner Azure architect |
| 15:30 | Workshop output review + next steps | PM + all |
| 16:30 | Close | Sponsor |

**Outputs**

- Wave plan (draft)
- HCX readiness checklist with gaps
- DR strategy + chosen mechanism
- Landing zone gap list + remediation backlog

---

## Workshop pack deliverable

A single PDF / SharePoint folder containing:
- Workshop slides
- Day-by-day notes
- All outputs above
- Action register
- Recommended next phase (Offering 1 / 2 / 3 + estimated effort)
